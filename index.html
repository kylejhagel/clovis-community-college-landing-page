Mechatronics Industrial Automation

Program Overview

Build practical skills for careers in advanced manufacturing, industrial automation, robotics, electronics, and equipment maintenance.

Clovis Community College's Mechatronics Industrial Automation program combines hands-on technical training with pathways to a certificate or associate degree. Students also prepare for industry-recognized NIMS Industrial Technology Maintenance credentials (external PDF) while developing skills used by employers throughout the Central Valley.

What You'll Learn

  • Industrial automation
  • Robotics and controls
  • Electrical and electronic systems
  • Manufacturing technology
  • Industrial maintenance and troubleshooting

Career Opportunities

Where This Program Can Take You

Mechatronics training prepares students for technical careers that keep automated, electrical, mechanical, and manufacturing systems operating across the Central Valley and beyond.

Field Service Technician

Install, maintain, troubleshoot, and repair automated equipment and industrial systems at customer and work sites.

Industrial Maintenance Technician

Inspect, maintain, and troubleshoot mechanical, electrical, and automated systems used in industrial facilities.

Maintenance Mechanic

Maintain and repair machinery, production equipment, and mechanical systems to support safe and reliable operations.

Maintenance Repair Mechanic

Diagnose equipment problems and perform repairs that keep industrial and manufacturing systems working efficiently.
