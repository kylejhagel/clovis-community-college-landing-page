Mechatronics Industrial Automation

Program Overview

Build practical skills for careers in advanced manufacturing, industrial automation, robotics, electronics, and equipment maintenance.

Clovis Community College's Mechatronics Industrial Automation program combines hands-on technical training with pathways to a certificate or associate degree. Students also prepare for industry-recognized NIMS Industrial Technology Maintenance credentials while developing skills used by employers throughout the Central Valley.

What You'll Learn

  • Industrial automation
  • Robotics and controls
  • Electrical and electronic systems
  • Manufacturing technology
  • Industrial maintenance and troubleshooting
