Mechatronics Industrial Automation

Program Overview

Build practical skills for careers in advanced manufacturing, industrial automation, robotics, electronics, and equipment maintenance.

Clovis Community College's Mechatronics Industrial Automation program combines hands-on technical training with pathways to a certificate or associate degree. Students also prepare for industry-recognized NIMS Industrial Technology Maintenance credentials (external PDF) while developing skills used by employers throughout the Central Valley.

What You'll Learn

  • Industrial automation
  • Robotics and controls
  • Electrical and electronic systems
  • Manufacturing technology
  • Industrial maintenance and troubleshooting

Career Opportunities

Where This Program Can Take You

Mechatronics training prepares students for technical careers that keep automated, electrical, mechanical, and manufacturing systems operating across the Central Valley and beyond.

Field Service Technician

Install, maintain, troubleshoot, and repair automated equipment and industrial systems at customer and work sites.

Industrial Maintenance Technician

Inspect, maintain, and troubleshoot mechanical, electrical, and automated systems used in industrial facilities.

Maintenance Mechanic

Maintain and repair machinery, production equipment, and mechanical systems to support safe and reliable operations.

Maintenance Repair Mechanic

Diagnose equipment problems and perform repairs that keep industrial and manufacturing systems working efficiently.

Start Building Your Skills August 10

Begin the Mechatronics Industrial Automation program with hands-on courses in maintenance, mechanical systems, hydraulics, pneumatics, and electricity.

Build a foundation in industrial maintenance practices, documentation, safety, inspection, and troubleshooting.

What You'll Learn

  • Preventive maintenance and technical documentation
  • Reading technical drawings and maintenance information
  • Safe use of hand tools and equipment
  • Inspection and troubleshooting practices

Course Outcome

Students who successfully complete the required safety training receive an OSHA 10-hour course completion card.

Learn how mechanical power-transmission systems operate and how to maintain them safely.

What You'll Learn

  • Chains, belts, bearings, and gear trains
  • Alignment, lubrication, and measuring tools
  • Mechanical troubleshooting
  • Safe maintenance practices

Develop practical skills for understanding, operating, and troubleshooting industrial hydraulic systems.

What You'll Learn

  • Hydraulic schematics and system components
  • Flow, pressure, and lubrication
  • Hydraulic troubleshooting
  • Fluid-power safety

Work with compressed-air systems and the components that control motion in automated equipment.

What You'll Learn

  • Compressors, valves, and cylinders
  • Air motors and pneumatic logic devices
  • System operation and troubleshooting
  • Pneumatic safety

Build core electrical skills used to understand and maintain industrial control systems.

What You'll Learn

  • AC and DC electrical fundamentals
  • Voltage, current, resistance, and power
  • Electrical schematics, relays, and motor starters
  • Switches, sensors, indicators, and basic motor controls
